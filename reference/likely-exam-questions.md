# Likely Exam Questions

Use this file to collect possible exam questions as you revise.

## Questions

1. What problem does Claude solve in enterprise solution architecture?
2. What is AI Fluency?
3. Why is AI Fluency broader than prompt engineering?
4. What are the Four Ds of AI Fluency?
5. What does Delegation mean in human-AI collaboration?
6. What does Description mean when working with AI?
7. Why is Discernment important?
8. What does Diligence add to responsible AI use?
9. Why should AI be treated as a collaborative partner instead of only a tool?
10. Why does AI Fluency matter for solution architects?

## Short Answers

1. Claude helps teams build AI-assisted workflows, automate knowledge work, analyze documents, support users, and integrate reasoning capabilities into business systems.
2. AI Fluency is the ability to collaborate with AI effectively, responsibly, and confidently using communication, judgment, evaluation, and accountability.
3. Prompt engineering focuses on instructions, while AI Fluency includes deciding when to use AI, how to communicate, how to evaluate outputs, and how to remain responsible.
4. The Four Ds are Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence.
5. Delegation means deciding which parts of a task belong to humans, AI, or a human-AI workflow.
6. Description means giving clear context, instructions, constraints, examples, and expected output format to the AI system.
7. Discernment matters because AI outputs may be wrong, incomplete, biased, or not suitable for the goal.
8. Diligence ensures AI use is responsible, transparent, ethical, and accountable.
9. Treating AI as a partner encourages creative problem-solving while keeping humans responsible for direction and final judgment.
10. Solution architects need AI Fluency to design AI-enabled systems that balance capability, human oversight, risk, governance, and business value.
