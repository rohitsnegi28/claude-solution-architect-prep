## Short Answers

1. Claude helps teams build AI-assisted workflows, automate knowledge work, analyze documents, support users, and integrate reasoning capabilities into business systems.
2. AI Fluency is the ability to collaborate with AI effectively, responsibly, and confidently using communication, judgment, evaluation, and accountability.
3. Prompt engineering focuses on instructions, while AI Fluency includes deciding when to use AI, how to communicate, how to evaluate outputs, and how to remain responsible.
4. The Four Ds are Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence.
5. Delegation means deciding which parts of a task belong to humans, AI, or a human-AI workflow.
6. Description means giving clear context, instructions, constraints, examples, and expected output format to the AI system.
7. Discernment matters because AI outputs may be wrong, incomplete, biased, or not suitable for the goal.
8. Diligence ensures AI use is responsible, transparent, ethical, and accountable.
9. Treating AI as a partner encourages creative problem-solving while keeping humans responsible for direction and final judgment.
10. Solution architects need AI Fluency to design AI-enabled systems that balance capability, human oversight, risk, governance, and business value.
11. The three modes are automation, augmentation, and agency.
12. Automation executes a clear task; augmentation supports human thinking on complex or creative work.
13. Agency means AI acts more independently toward a goal set by a human, under boundaries and oversight.
14. Traditional AI usually classifies or predicts; generative AI creates new content.
15. An LLM is a generative AI model trained to understand and generate human language.
16. The three breakthroughs are transformer architecture, abundant data, and powerful compute such as GPUs and TPUs.
17. Pre-training builds broad capability from large datasets; fine-tuning shapes model behavior for helpfulness, safety, or specific tasks.
18. A context window is the amount of information the model can process at one time.
19. A hallucination is a confident but incorrect or unsupported AI output.
20. A knowledge cutoff is the point after which the model may not know new information unless connected to current sources.
21. LLMs are probabilistic, so the same prompt can produce different valid-looking responses.
22. The three elements are problem awareness, platform awareness, and task delegation.
23. Problem awareness means understanding goals, domain context, risks, and success criteria before using AI.
24. Platform awareness means understanding the strengths, limits, and best-fit uses of different AI systems.
25. Product description defines what to create, process description defines how to work, and performance description defines how the AI should behave.
26. Give context, show examples, specify constraints, break tasks into steps, ask Claude to think first, and define role/style/tone.
27. Few-shot prompting means giving examples so the AI can follow the desired pattern.
28. Breaking tasks into steps reduces ambiguity and improves output quality for complex work.
29. Product discernment checks the final output, process discernment checks reasoning steps, and performance discernment checks interaction quality.
30. Description sets expectations, discernment evaluates whether they were met, and feedback improves the next response.
31. Diligence is the responsible, ethical, safe, transparent, and accountable use of AI.
32. The three pillars are creation diligence, transparency diligence, and deployment diligence.
33. Creation diligence means intentionally choosing AI systems and understanding training, data ownership, privacy, and task suitability.
34. Transparency diligence means being open about when and how AI contributed to work.
35. Deployment diligence means verifying facts, checking bias and quality, and taking responsibility before using or sharing AI-assisted output.
36. The user or organization decides how AI output is used, so accountability remains with them.
37. Delegation decides the work split, Description guides the AI, Discernment evaluates the result, and Diligence ensures responsible use.
38. AI Fluency is a practice because AI systems keep evolving and users must continuously improve their judgment, communication, evaluation, and responsibility.
