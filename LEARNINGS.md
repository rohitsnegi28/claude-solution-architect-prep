# Learnings

Use this as the beginner-friendly revision map. Detailed notes stay in `course-modules/`; this file keeps the durable mental model.

## Core Idea

AI Fluency means knowing how to work with AI effectively, safely, ethically, and confidently. It is broader than prompt engineering because it includes deciding when to use AI, guiding it well, checking its work, and staying responsible for the final outcome.

## Main Frameworks

### Three Modes of AI Use

- Automation: AI performs a clear, repeatable task.
- Augmentation: AI works as a thinking partner.
- Agency: AI acts more independently toward a goal under human oversight.

Higher autonomy requires stronger oversight.

### Four Ds of AI Fluency

- Delegation: decide who should do what.
- Description: explain what is needed and how AI should work.
- Discernment: evaluate the output, reasoning, and collaboration quality.
- Diligence: use AI responsibly, transparently, and accountably.

## Generative AI Basics

- Traditional AI usually classifies, predicts, detects, or recommends.
- Generative AI creates new content such as text, code, images, plans, summaries, and explanations.
- Large Language Models generate language by learning patterns from large-scale training.
- Modern generative AI depends on transformer architecture, large datasets, and powerful compute.

## Key Risks to Remember

- Knowledge cutoff: the model may not know current facts.
- Hallucination: the model can produce confident but unsupported answers.
- Context window limits: the model can only process a limited amount at once.
- Non-determinism: similar prompts may produce different outputs.
- Accountability: AI assistance does not remove human responsibility.

## One-Minute Revision

AI Fluency is a practice. Use AI where it fits, describe the work clearly, check results critically, and own the outcome.
