# Lesson 03: The 4D Framework

## Topic Overview

The 4D Framework is the foundation of AI Fluency. It gives users four durable competencies for working with AI systems effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely. These competencies remain useful even as AI tools and models change.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, I should be able to:

- List and explain the Four Ds.
- Describe how each competency supports human-AI collaboration.
- Apply the Four Ds to solution design and enterprise AI use cases.
- Explain why the framework is more durable than specific prompting tricks.

## The Four Ds

### 1. Delegation

Delegation is deciding what work should be done by the human, what can be supported by AI, and what can be entrusted to AI under oversight.

Key questions:

- What is the goal?
- What parts require human expertise?
- What parts are repetitive or pattern-based?
- What risks appear if AI gets it wrong?

### 2. Description

Description is the ability to communicate clearly with AI. It includes explaining the task, context, desired output, process, constraints, examples, and interaction style.

Key questions:

- What does the AI need to know?
- What format should the output follow?
- What constraints or assumptions matter?
- How should the AI behave as a collaborator?

### 3. Discernment

Discernment is critically evaluating AI outputs and behavior. It involves checking accuracy, relevance, reasoning quality, usefulness, and fit for purpose.

Key questions:

- Is the output correct?
- Is it complete and relevant?
- Does the reasoning make sense?
- Is further human review needed?

### 4. Diligence

Diligence is using AI responsibly. It includes fairness, bias control, privacy protection, transparency, and accountability for the final work.

Key questions:

- Is sensitive data protected?
- Could the output create harm or unfairness?
- Is AI use transparent where needed?
- Who owns the final decision?

## Architecture / Solution Design Connection

In enterprise AI systems, the Four Ds map naturally to design responsibilities:

- Delegation maps to task allocation and workflow design.
- Description maps to prompt design, interface design, and input quality.
- Discernment maps to evaluation, testing, validation, and human review.
- Diligence maps to governance, compliance, privacy, and accountability.

## Exam-Focused Takeaways

- Four Ds = Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence.
- Delegation decides who or what should do the work.
- Description guides the AI clearly.
- Discernment evaluates the output and process.
- Diligence ensures responsible and accountable use.
- The framework supports long-term AI collaboration, not only short-term prompt improvement.
