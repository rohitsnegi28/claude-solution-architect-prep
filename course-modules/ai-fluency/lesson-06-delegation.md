# Lesson 06: Delegation

## Topic Overview

Delegation is the AI Fluency competency focused on deciding which tasks to do yourself and which tasks to entrust to AI. It is not passive automation. It is a deliberate choice about how to combine human expertise with AI strengths.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, I should be able to:

- Define delegation in AI collaboration.
- Explain the three elements of successful delegation.
- Decide when AI should automate, augment, or act with agency.
- Identify tasks that require human judgment.

## Three Elements of Successful Delegation

### 1. Problem Awareness

Problem awareness means clearly understanding your goals, project vision, constraints, risks, and domain context before involving AI.

Important idea: Effective collaborators are domain experts first and AI users second.

Questions to ask:

- What am I trying to achieve?
- What does success look like?
- What domain knowledge matters?
- What are the risks if the answer is wrong?

### 2. Platform Awareness

Platform awareness means understanding that different AI systems have different strengths, weaknesses, interfaces, and constraints. Users should experiment hands-on to learn which tools fit which tasks.

Questions to ask:

- Which model or platform is suitable?
- Does the task need current information?
- Does it require file processing, coding, images, or tool use?
- What are the model's limitations?

### 3. Task Delegation

Task delegation means thoughtfully distributing work after understanding the problem and the platform.

Options include:

- Automate repetitive, clear tasks.
- Augment complex thinking and creative work.
- Keep high-risk judgment with humans.
- Use agents only with clear goals, boundaries, and monitoring.

## Architecture / Solution Design Connection

Delegation is central to AI solution architecture. It affects workflow design, risk management, human review, and automation boundaries.

A solution architect should define:

- Which steps AI performs.
- Which steps humans review.
- Which steps require approval.
- Which failures need escalation.
- Which tasks should never be fully delegated.

## Exam-Focused Takeaways

- Delegation begins with understanding the problem.
- Domain expertise matters more than tool excitement.
- Platform awareness helps match tasks to AI capabilities.
- Good delegation balances automation, augmentation, agency, and human judgment.
- AI collaboration is deliberate decision-making, not blind outsourcing.
