# AI Fluency Learning Notes

## Plain Definition

AI Fluency is the ability to use AI as a capable but imperfect collaborator. It combines skill, knowledge, values, judgment, and accountability.

## What To Remember

- Prompting is only one part of AI Fluency.
- Good AI use starts before the prompt: decide whether AI is suitable.
- Good AI use continues after the output: verify, improve, and own the result.

## The Four Ds

1. Delegation: choose the right work split.
2. Description: communicate clearly.
3. Discernment: evaluate critically.
4. Diligence: use responsibly.

## Architecture Connection

For solution architects, AI Fluency helps design workflows with clear boundaries, human oversight, verification steps, and governance expectations.
