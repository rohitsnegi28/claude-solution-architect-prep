# Quick Review

## Memory Hooks

- AI Fluency = use AI well and responsibly.
- Four Ds = Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence.
- Three modes = Automation, Augmentation, Agency.
- LLM risks = cutoff, hallucination, context limit, variability.
- Diligence = creation, transparency, deployment.

## Interview-Style Questions

1. Why is AI Fluency broader than prompt engineering?
2. How do automation, augmentation, and agency differ?
3. What are the Four Ds, and how do they work together?
4. Why does increasing AI autonomy increase oversight needs?
5. What should a solution architect check before deploying AI-assisted output?

## Fast Answers

1. Because it includes task choice, communication, evaluation, ethics, and accountability.
2. Automation completes clear tasks; augmentation supports human thinking; agency acts more independently within boundaries.
3. Delegation decides the work split, Description guides the AI, Discernment checks quality, and Diligence ensures responsible use.
4. More autonomy creates more risk, so boundaries, validation, and human accountability matter more.
5. Accuracy, bias, privacy, suitability, transparency, and business impact.
